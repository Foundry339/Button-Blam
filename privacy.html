Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Button Blam ("the site," "we," "us") is a small, mostly pointless website you can click a button on. This policy explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your click count, achievements, streaks, and other stats are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or any server. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser or device will reset your progress.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads served by third party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third party vendor cookies generally at aboutads.info.

Payments and donations

The "Feed the button" link takes you to a payment page hosted by Stripe. We do not receive, see, or store your card details. Any payment information you provide is handled directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Button Blam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes, for example if a leaderboard or account system is added in the future. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
